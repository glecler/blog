Introduction

I've been wanting to create my own blog for a long time now. I have no intention to make this an impressive website but simply be able to write about things I like and things I work on.

Pure HTML + css seems like a breathe of fresh air to me, in a time when claude can easily create everything without any effort. It also gives me some outlet to think with my brain, produce something I fully own, without complexity, and express some creativity.

I intend to discuss many things here, my interests being (electronic) music, extreme sports, science, geopolitics.

I have also recently co founded my company, statsIQ, that tries to make a place for itself in the esports industry. I'm continuously learning new things, and am a very opinionated individual, so I have a lot of things to say.
